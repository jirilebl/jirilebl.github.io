Fractal Animations

A few small interactive animations showing how some classic fractals are built up step by step. Each runs right in the browser: pick the number of iterations and the speed, then press Start (or step through one iteration at a time).

  • Koch Snowflake
    Start with an equilateral triangle and repeatedly replace the middle third of every edge with two sides of a smaller equilateral triangle pointing outward. Watch the perimeter grow without bound while the enclosed area stays finite.
  • Sierpinski Triangle by Removal
    Start with a filled triangle and at each step remove the middle triangle (the one formed by joining the midpoints of the sides) from every remaining triangle. What is left in the limit is the Sierpinski triangle.
  • Sierpinski Triangle via a Chaos Game
    A completely different route to the same fractal: start at a point, then repeatedly jump halfway toward a randomly chosen vertex of a triangle and mark where you land. Despite the randomness, the Sierpinski triangle emerges.
